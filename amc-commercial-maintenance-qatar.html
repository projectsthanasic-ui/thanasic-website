Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

An AMC can set out planned preventive visits, the equipment or areas covered, response arrangements and the responsibilities of each party. The right scope depends on the property, asset list, service frequency and operating requirements.

Preventive visits

Discuss a planned schedule for inspection and routine maintenance of agreed equipment.

Breakdown enquiries

Define how urgent maintenance requests should be reported and how response arrangements will be handled.

Scope and reporting

Agree covered assets, exclusions, parts arrangements and any service records or reports required.

Commercial property maintenance

Thanasic welcomes enquiries from offices, shops, villas, compounds and other facilities requiring coordinated AC and general maintenance. Larger projects should include a clear asset list, site location, access details and preferred maintenance schedule.

What to include in an AMC enquiry

  • Property name and location
  • Number and type of AC units or other assets
  • Required services: AC, electrical, plumbing or general maintenance
  • Preferred visit frequency and contract duration
  • Any response-time or reporting requirements

Parts, consumables, specialist works and emergency arrangements should be clearly agreed in the written quotation or contract. Contact Thanasic to discuss a suitable scope.
