Common Electrical Faults We Fix in Al Khor

Heat and dust affect electrical systems as much as air conditioning. Distribution boards in villas and compounds often run hot, breakers trip under load, and wiring insulation ages faster in the climate. Most of our electrical calls in Al Khor are about intermittent power loss, circuits that trip, sockets and switches that need replacing, and lighting upgrades to LED throughout a property.

Every visit starts with fault finding: we isolate the problem to its source — a breaker, a circuit, a connection or a load — and confirm with you what it will cost before work begins. We do not simply reset panels and leave; the goal is a fault that does not come back.
