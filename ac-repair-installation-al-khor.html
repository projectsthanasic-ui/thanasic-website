AC troubleshooting and repair

Air-conditioning problems can have several causes, including restricted airflow, dirty filters or coils, blocked drainage, electrical faults, thermostat issues or refrigerant leaks. A proper inspection helps identify the likely cause before repair work is agreed.

AC not cooling

Enquire about a cooling-performance check, airflow inspection and system diagnostics.

AC installation

Discuss installation or replacement requirements for split, window and other supported AC systems. Site conditions and equipment details need to be confirmed.

Gas checks and refilling

Low refrigerant may indicate a leak. Ask for leak inspection and pressure checks before any refrigerant top-up is considered.

AC repair for homes and businesses

Share the AC type, brand, symptoms and property location when contacting Thanasic. This helps the team understand the job and confirm whether a visit can be arranged. Service availability for areas outside Al Khor should be confirmed before booking.

  • Water leakage or blocked drain
